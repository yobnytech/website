QueueOne (Digital queueing platform / virtual queuing)

What it is
QueueOne is described as a digital / virtual queuing platform that helps businesses/institutions provide queues to end-users who can join from anywhere, with real-time updates about queue progress.
  • Tools to create and manage “digital queues”
  • User-facing experience aims to let people join remotely and come to the point-of-service only when needed
  • Position and queue progress are presented as real-time updates
Source: QueueOne project page on Devpost describing “What it does”.
Stated motivation / problem
  • Queues are “inevitable” but time-consuming and can negatively impact business revenue
  • Goal: convert physical queues into “digital avatars”
  • Emphasis on real-time guidance and reducing crowding (COVID-era “new normal” language)
Source: Devpost “Inspiration” section.
How it was built (technical details shared)
  • Backend: Python APIs and micro-services architecture
  • Deployment: AWS; GCP used “as needed” (Firebase mentioned), plus Terraform
  • Platform type: SaaS with a self-service dashboard
  • Apps: both mobile and web apps for end users
  • Stack/keywords listed: AI, IoT, ML, Python, React
Source: Devpost “How I built it” and “Built With”.
Pilots / deployments mentioned
  • MVP launch with the Election Commission of India during General Elections 2019. Here rolled out digital queue for voter's of one Polling Booth in Bangalore South consituency
  • “Workec with Industry leaders” Apollo (healthcare) and Future group (retail)
Source: Devpost “Accomplishments…” section.

Next / future features mentioned
